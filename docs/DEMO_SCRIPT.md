# Demo video

**Watch:** https://youtu.be/u3avKfEDRxQ (2:17)

Genuine footage of the live app at https://fundcart-ai.vercel.app: real product results, real AI output and real PayPal Sandbox payments (test money). Narration uses an AI-generated voice. Captions are burned in.

## Storyboard

| Section | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | FundCart AI home page | Meet FundCart AI, where smart shopping meets community support. Finding the right product is only half the challenge. Sometimes the bigger question is how to afford it. |
| AI shopping | Maya's request and the agent activity panel | Maya needs a laptop for university. She describes it in her own words: under seven hundred dollars, with two hundred already saved. FundCart's AI understands her budget and purpose, and searches a live product catalog. |
| Recommendations | Real products, AI explanations, funding gaps | Every result is a real product, with a live price and retailer. The AI explains which laptops suit her studies, and FundCart works out exactly how far her savings go. The top pick, a Dell Chromebook at five hundred and fifty dollars, leaves a three hundred and fifty dollar gap. |
| Budget planning | Side-by-side comparison, "Fund this" | She compares her options side by side. Then, instead of giving up, she chooses Fund this. |
| Fundraising | Campaign builder, AI draft, publish | FundCart turns the gap into a campaign. The goal comes straight from the live price, never from the AI. The AI drafts an honest story, and Maya can edit every word before publishing. With one click, her campaign is live, with a link to share. |
| Supporter | Public campaign page | A friend opens the link and chooses an amount. FundCart never accepts more than the campaign still needs. |
| PayPal contribution | PayPal Sandbox checkout window | Payment happens in PayPal. This is the PayPal Sandbox, so it is test money and nothing real is charged. The supporter signs in and approves the payment inside PayPal. |
| Confirmed progress | "Contribution verified", goal reached | Progress only moves when PayPal confirms the payment, for the exact amount. Maya's goal is reached. |
| Dashboard | Creator dashboard and payment ledger | Maya's dashboard lists every PayPal payment for her campaign, with its status and reference. The purchase is now unlocked, but nothing is bought automatically. |
| Purchase | Separate PayPal Sandbox checkout | Maya ticks an explicit approval, then pays the store in a separate PayPal checkout. The AI prepares. Only a person approves money. |
| History | Purchase completed, campaign history | The purchase is confirmed, and every step is recorded in the campaign's history, including PayPal's own payment notifications. A payment is never counted twice. |
| How it works | How it works page | Behind the scenes, cooperating AI agents handle understanding, research, comparison and budgeting, while PayPal handles every payment. Shopping help and community support, in one place. |
| Ending | Title card | FundCart AI. Find what you need. Fund what you dream. |

The Sandbox payments in the video: a $350.00 contribution to the campaign, and a separate $550.00 purchase paid to the demo store account.
