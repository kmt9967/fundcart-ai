## Eligibility

- Individuals at the age of majority, teams, and organizations.
- Excluded jurisdictions include Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea and other OFAC comprehensively sanctioned countries.
- Pakistan is **not** excluded.
- Devpost shows our account is registered: the hackathon page shows the "Edit project / Create project" controls and "My projects".

## What to build

- An application that integrates the **PayPal developer platform (free Sandbox)** plus an **AI tool, model or platform**.
- It must meaningfully use at least one PayPal technology and meaningfully incorporate AI.
- It must be a working prototype, sufficiently documented, and compliant with the Official Rules.
- New or existing projects are allowed, provided meaningful progress is made during the hackathon.

## What to submit

- A text description of the features and functionality.
- **A functional demo.** Either clear setup and run instructions in the repo, or a hosted demo URL. A mockup, static prototype or non-functional demo does not qualify.
- Tools: which AI model or service was used, and how.
- **A public repo** on GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket. It must be open source, with the license visible in the About section.
- **A demo video under 3 minutes.**
  - It must show the project working on its intended device.
  - It must be on YouTube and public.
  - No third-party trademarks or copyrighted music without permission.

## Judging criteria (equally weighted)

1. **Technological Implementation:** how thoroughly the PayPal platform and AI tools are used; a working, non-trivial implementation.
2. **Design:** a complete, coherent product experience.
3. **Potential Impact:** a credible case for a real problem and a real audience.
4. **Innovation / Idea:** creative and novel.
5. **Presentation:** the video shows end-to-end working; problem, audience and why it matters are clear.

Ties are broken by criterion order (Technological Implementation first).
